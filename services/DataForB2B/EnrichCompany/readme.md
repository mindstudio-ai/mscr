# DataForB2B — Enrich Company

Enrich a single company from its name, domain, LinkedIn URL or id. Returns full firmographic data including employee head-count / size, locations, funding rounds, investors and growth signals.

## Configuration
- **Company Identifier** *(required)*: a universal name/slug (e.g. `google`), a full or short LinkedIn company URL, or an encoded id (`org_...`).

## Output
Returns the enriched company object.

## Notes
- This connector consumes credits from your DataForB2B account.
